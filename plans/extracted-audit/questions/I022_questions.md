## Method notes (not questions)

- **Screenshots after `advance_frame`, `compare_runs` or a replay can show a strip or black, because the emulator stops at scanline 1 to 5 of the next frame.** `step_scanline` with `count` 235 then `take_screenshot` with `layers` gives the settled image in every case of this run. (This refines `OQ-I020-6`; the black frames at the end of the Trial Room replay were this effect, not a build difference.)
- **Do not watch `$1C6A` from frame 0.** The first `compare_runs` of this run watched the location id (`$1C6A`) and reported one divergent frame (frame 1: 14 against 48): it is uninitialised power-on WRAM that the boot code overwrites on the next frame. Without that watch the 3,677-frame replay is identical.
- **Equal CPU registers in the replays are not a requirement.** After the replays the two builds have the same `A`, `X`, `SP`, `DBR` values and `pc` in different places inside the wait loop of bank 2 (62,728 against 61,512 in the menu run); the code of bank 2 is placed differently by the packer.
