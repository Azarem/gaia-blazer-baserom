# I057 open questions (docs/code/data-tables.md: the data tables)

IDs are scoped to this iteration and cover only new findings. Nothing here is written into names, `blocks.json`, `overrides.json` or the docs as fact. Mode: R = static read only, R+S = read, then confirm with a short Mesen2 run, S = needs a runtime run, D = needs a decision. Tag DISCREPANCY marks a mismatch between the ROM and the walkthrough or another document; GAP marks something the static reading could not decide. Tool column names the `user-mesen2-mcp-dev` tools. Addresses are `$bank:address` (LoROM). Record numbers are the `;NN` row numbers of `LairRecords` in hex.

The walkthrough was checked for every game term the document uses: the EXP ladder (all 49 values), the HP gauge colours, the six boss lairs (Metal Mantis, Elemental Statues, Floating Skull, Poseidon, Tin Doll, Demon Falcon), the freed Chief and the five dream-room switches. The ROM agrees except for the four boss HP values and the dictionary spelling, which stay as `OQ-I033-1`, `OQ-I010-2` and `OQ-I033-7`; no new DISCREPANCY row is needed.

Settled statically by this iteration (no new row, the note text was corrected through `edits/I057.json`):

- `OQ-I033-9`: the reader of `CircleHalfWidths` is `BuildSoulOrbCircleWindow`; the shape is the circular window of radius `$40` around the soul orb that the four Soul effects cut into the screen.
- `OQ-I015-2`: the 15 filler rows of `LairRecords` have location `$63`, which is a hole (zero word) in the profile, chest, exit, plate and `LocationAreaScene` tables, so no scene can load it; the rows only keep the numbering.
- `OQ-I015-6` is confirmed by a second count: nine meta rows (world 0 `$09`; world 1 `$34`, `$36`, `$3B`, `$3E`; world 2 `$14`, `$25`, `$27`, `$33`) are named by no placement row and no lair record; the other worlds use every row.
- `OQ-I033-12` is narrowed: `+18`, `+1A` and `+1C` are zero in all 420 rows, so a reader could not tell the fields apart from padding; the width is still unverified.
