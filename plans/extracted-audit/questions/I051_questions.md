# I051 open questions (docs/code/vram-dma-hdma-palettes.md: VRAM, DMA, HDMA and palettes)

IDs are scoped to this iteration and cover only new findings. One DISCREPANCY row (OQ-I051-1): the note of `RunSoulOfLightEffect` named the wrong place for the dark area; the walkthrough (`items.md`, `03-greenwood.md`) is the source of truth and the note is corrected in `edits/I051.json`.
