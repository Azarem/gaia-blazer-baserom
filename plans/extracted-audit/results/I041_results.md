## Findings and corrections

- **`common` is the group `actor_shared`.** It holds helper code that other actor files reach from three banks. Nothing in the three files is a ROM actor root of its own except the palette loop (65 meta entries) and the Master (one meta type per world); the rest is entered by JSL or by a pushed RTL address.
- **The aimed shot (new).** Eight shot scripts (Blue Torch, Fire Man, Fire Bat, the castle dungeon and tower statues, the Bouncing Ball, the World of Evil pop-up turret, the Tin Doll) set Y to their animation id and RTL into `cm_enemy_aimed_shot_body_002D67`. The body spawns an invisible mover just before the shot (`COP [85]`; the child's `$3C` is the spawner, the spawner's `$3E` the child); the mover fixes one of eight cases from the signs of dx and dy and the larger axis (tie goes to y) and steps the major axis every frame and the minor axis every N-th frame, N = floor(major / minor). The mover ends when its `$3C` no longer equals `$30` (shot removed, shot wrote its flags word into `$30`, or a newer mover was inserted). The Purple Flower's bee uses the mover alone.
- **`OQ-I035-10` settled.** `CPY #$06AA` after `COP [85]` is the pool-full value `PopFreeSlot` returns; the note and the comment of the bee and the block note of `gw_lostside_marsh_enemies` are corrected (`I041c`).
- **`OQ-I030-1` settled statically.** Move sets `scene_next = 9`, scene 9 of area 0 being the Up Above hub A00S09 with the map walker; the table `cm_data_master_move_hub_positions_0076D3` gives each world the start stop of that world (the walker adds 16 to y, as `SpawnPlacementList` does). The notes of `shared_choice_prompt_texts` are corrected (`I041c`). Runtime confirmation: `OQ-I041-2`.
- **The Master (new).** No sprite, 'A voice came from above'; heals on arrival (`COP [37]`), polls the hero's tile: (7,4) opens the menu (Record / Quit, plus Move once flag `$1B00` is set), (7,6) is the arrival tile (comfort text after a death, then the one-time stone hint with flag `$0602`, which no other script uses). Record saves behind a pad block of `$7FC0` that it frees again; answering No to 'continue?' runs the 'Rest well' ending with `$7F80` that is never freed (`OQ-I041-1`).
- **Fairy gem and captive helpers.** The bank 03 setup is called from 12 actor files; the idle loop exists three times with identical code (bank 03, bank 04, the St. Elles copy `w2_obj_fairy_idle_loop_0FA945`). The two plea sets pick one of three texts by the low 2 bits of `rng_state+1` (0, 1, and 2 / 3 together). The bank 04 strings 02249A to 022507 are the two Mountain of Souls gems' texts; `asciistring_02251E` is the common tail of many texts of that world.
- **Names versus walkthrough.** The Master is not named in the ROM; the project name follows the walkthrough ('the Master'). No DISCREPANCY.
- **Mnemonics:** none added (no new evidence for an unnamed RAM cell; `warp_dest_*`, `joy_block_mask`, `world_index`, `player_died` already exist).
- **No ad-hoc scripts.** Names, notes and comments only through `npm run names` and `npm run merge` (dry-run first); the offsets of the names spec came from the line-tracked extraction.
