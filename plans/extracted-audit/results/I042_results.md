## Findings and corrections

- **A merge needs the cut to be visible to the verifier.** Fall-through at a file boundary is invisible to it, so every `split-part` cut was checked for a terminator (`RTS`, `RTL`, `RTI`, `JMP`, `BRA`, `BRL`) before it: offsets 2612, 3880, 72152 and 72599 all pass.
- **New hard pin.** The loader cannot give up `InitHardwareAndClearWram` (`OQ-I042-7`, in the loader block note since `OQ-I011-10`). The first plan split it away and the rebuild failed with 'Relative out of range in ScanAssetCommands: BRA loc_0139E6'.
- **Name correction.** `MarkHeldLairTilesSolid` was inaccurate: tile type `$01` is the top-left collision quadrant only. Renamed to `SetHeldLairTileType` in the same step. The sine wave mover variants are named by the flip state that `CopA5_JumpByFlipState` dispatches on (`NoFlip`, `FlipX`, `FlipY`, `FlipXY`), not by direction.
- **Stale cross references repaired** in `I042b_notes.json` (talk routines now in `lib_field_talk`, `SetEnemyGauge` in `engine_combat_hits`, `EnableDisplayAndNmi` in `lib_system_helpers`, two block names that never existed: `lib_027781_part3` and `common_actor_0066C6`). The old combat and tile collision sections of the scheduler note moved word for word to the notes of `engine_combat_hits` and `engine_tile_collision`.
- **Old off-by-one.** The previous scheduler note ended at SNES `$92AD`; the file's last offset 4782 is `$00:92AE`.
- **Left alone on purpose.** `lib_text_render`, `lib_scene_asset_loader`, `lib_menus` and `lib_tile_triggers_and_lair_return` are at or over the 1,500-line guide size; `player_core` and `engine_spawn_lairs_death_drops` mix subsystems and are candidates for a later wave (`OQ-I042-8`); bank 1F blocks keep their names.
- **No DISCREPANCY** with the walkthrough; nine open questions in `questions/I042_questions.md` (`OQ-I042-1` .. `OQ-I042-9`).
