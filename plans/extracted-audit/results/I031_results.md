## Findings and corrections

- **`Multiply8x8` factors (checked against the code).** `PixelToMapIndex` and `BuildTileTypeMap` pass the first factor in the low byte of A and the second in B (the high byte); the notes say so, and for `PixelToMapIndex` B is the high byte of dp$18 left by `LDA $18`. The XBA/TAY in `BuildTileTypeMap` gives pages x `$100` only while the product fits one byte (at most 64 pages in the shipped maps); the note records the limit.
- **`docs/wram-memory-map.md` corrected (OQ-I031-4, DISCREPANCY against a project document).** `player_exp` and `player_exp_next` are eight BCD digits in two words, not 24-bit: `AddExp` carries into `$1B7A` and caps both words, `code_002F56` loads the threshold as two words, and the walkthrough's EXP maximum is 99,999,999. The mnemonics `player_exp+2` and `player_exp_next+2` now print in the code.
- **`transition_type` is cleared after the in transition**, so one type drives both halves of a scene change. The scripts use only 4 (23 `Cop3C` uses, mosaic out and in) and `$80` (return trips, iris), and 0 by default; the other ladder entries look unreachable (OQ-I031-6). The block note carries the full ladders and the mosaic and iris arithmetic, which agree with FM-A9 section 1.3.
- **Event flag `$FF` has a writer.** `InitFixedState` stores `$0080` into `$1A7D` (flag `$FF`) at every start-up; this is the 'who sets it' part of `OQ-I011-13`. Whether it is cleared later stays open (OQ-I031-5).
- **Floor hazards.** `IsPlayerOnMetatile` serves four scripts, each of which first tests an inventory entry with `Cop19` and then hurts with `Cop39`: Lostside `$80` / 1, Leo's lab `$F3` and `$E1` / 4 (both guarded by entry `$0A`, the Ice Armor of the walkthrough), Magridd's castle `$88` / 3 (entry `$0F`, Elemental Mail), St. Elles coast `$47` / 4 (a flag test, no item). The walkthrough agrees on the guarding items; the coast case is OQ-I031-2.
- **`PixelToMapIndex` adds the unmasked low byte of y** (OQ-I031-1): exact only for y with a low nibble of 0. `IsPlayerOnMetatile` masks it; `player_core` and `Cop28` do not.
- **`pause_latch` and the pause.** `WaitFrameForStart` decrements `frame_counter` to cancel the NMI increment, so the game clock stands still while paused. `APUIO0` receives `$F2` at pause and `$01` at resume (OQ-I013-6). Walkthrough `01-in-the-beginning.md` agrees that Start shows the lair count.
- **`ApplyPlayerWeaponPose` switches the animation data bank** (`$24` = `$0A` unarmed, `$0B` armed), not an animation id; the note and OQ-I031-3 say so. The earlier reading in FM-A9 (`$24 = $0A`) is the same fact, described as a bank.
- **Mask table in the sort bucket page.** `FillTileTypeMap` builds its 256-entry mask table at `$0200-$02FF`, which is the sort bucket page; `ResetSortBuckets` at the end of `InitSceneState` wipes it. The routine depends on DBR in `$00-$3F` (OQ-I013-13). `STA $cgram_buffer, X` with X from `$8000` writes `tile_type_map`, not the colour buffer: commented.
- **Save slot layout verified.** `+$000` `lair_state` (`$200`), `+$200` the image `$1A5E-$1C57` (`$1FA`), `+$3FA` sum, `+$3FC` xor, mirror at `+$1000` (`$3FE` bytes), `$70:1FFE` = last slot, `saved_scene` = `world_index + 1` (scene 1-7 of area 0 are the shrines in world order, `docs/areas-and-scenes.md`). Seeds `$3652`, `$1FD` words.
- **Callers corrected from FM-A9** (file names changed in the regroup): `SetBitInArray` is called from `lib_tile_triggers_and_lair_return` (5) and `engine_003005` (2); `AddExp` is called from `DrainPendingExp` and the two kill paths of `engine_scheduler_sprites_combat_collision`, not from `Cop36_AddExp` (which adds inline and has no script).
- **Dead code** stays as marked by `OQ-I013-12`: `FillSineWaveTables` (the three parameter cells are named from it, confidence C) and `ClearBitInArray`.
- **No new DISCREPANCY against the walkthrough.** The Elemental Mail / Elemental Armor spelling is the existing `OQ-I004-15`.
