## Findings and corrections

- **BRK has two sound bytes.** `HandleBrk` stores `BRK #n` in `$0398` when bit 6 of `n` is clear, and `n and $BF` in `$0399` when it is set; the NMI sends the word to `APUIO2` on even frames. Older text that says 'sound `$43`' means `BRK #$43` (`$0399 = $03`). The notes use the `BRK #n` form. What the driver does with the high byte is OQ-I024-1.
- **FM-A1 section 1.2 correction.** The title NMI hook (`UpdateTitleMode7`) builds a rotation matrix and writes it to the Mode 7 registers; it is not a block copy. `m7_angle` is read but never written in `extracted/`, so the title only zooms (OQ-I024-4). The matrix reaches the PPU one NMI late (the shadows are written before the new matrix is copied in).
- **`UnusedAnimWaitStep4`** is the step counter `$0482` of the `gv_front_end` sequence: an unused step of the title sequence (OQ-I024-6, with OQ-I006-7).
- **`RunLairRespawnFlipping`** is unreferenced (kept as a named routine, noted as unreferenced).
- **Death items must be equipped.** `CheckPlayerDeath` tests the inventory entries with Cop19, which needs bit 7 (equipped): Medical Herb `$38` heals, Strange Bottle `$39` and Magic Bell `$40` kill with the Gems kept, nothing zeroes the Gems (OQ-I006-1, -2).
- **Lair model written down:** `lair_state` (`$7F0203 + lair index`) is set to 0 only when the lair is cleared, so a half-cleared lair restarts with its full count after a scene change; the tile under the lair is `$FD` while intact and `$FE` (the green switch) after `SealLair` (OQ-I006-23).
- **Gem drops:** the 10/5/1 Gem pickup is chosen from `random_mod_result` against thresholds that grow with the world number and double with the Lucky Blade; `ApplyGemPickup` keys on the animation id.
- **Merge fix during the work:** one comment key (`{9987}`) pointed at a branch instead of `LDA ($4A)` (tag 9985); the `expect` guard caught it in the dry run.
- No new DISCREPANCY rows; `OQ-I005-1/2/3` and the I006 DISCREPANCY rows are cited by ID in the notes.
