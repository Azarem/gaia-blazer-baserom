## Findings and corrections

- **A mnemonic can change the ROM.** The first full gate gave SHA-1 `4802dc2f...`. Bisecting `us/mnemonics.json` showed the single entry `"7058": "player_name"` (`$1B92`) was the cause: `binary_list_014789` prints entry 2 as a pointer operand (`$player_name`), and the name makes the assembler emit different bytes than the numeric `$#1B92`. The entry was removed (the string at `$1B92` has no mnemonic; the WRAM doc and the notes say why) and the SHA-1 returned to `3148390e...`. Earlier batches kept the SHA-1, so none of their names was a pointer-table operand. Worth remembering for later mnemonics: a name inside a `$name` pointer-table entry is not text-only.
- **FM-A5 corrections** (the code disagrees):
  - `UpdateHud` returns at once for locations `$0F-$12`, so the heal and EXP drains are skipped there as well, not only the redraw (OQ-I027-2).
  - `ClearBg3Page` and `ClearBg3UpperHalf` set their upload bit with DBR = `$7F` (OQ-I027-1).
  - The HUD `[02:0]` and enemy gauge `[02:1]` strings are fixed six-tile labels of `binary_list_014789`, not the player or monster name; only entry 2 is the player name.
- **Call-site facts corrected in the notes while checking:** `ChooseMenuRowAtTable` is called from `engine_title_file_select` (not `engine_006B7F`); `Multiply8x8` has about 22 call sites; `DivideWordByByte` two (`ChooseFacingAnim`, `shared_actor_helpers_bank0`); `EnableNmi` 12 and `DisableNmi` 9.
- **Engine block note repaired:** after the rename `engine_boot_main_loop` read 'WaitVBlank (WaitVBlank)', 'DisableNmi (DisableNmi)' and 'EnableNmi (EnableNmi)'; the six `edit` operations fixed that and reworded the step 3 (button dispatch), step 7 (HUD skip) and the M-flag sentence.
- **Walkthrough check:** weapon minimum levels, strength and defence bonuses and the eight spell names match. Item name differences (`The Soul Blade`, `Emblem A..H`, `Red+Hot`, two items named `Leaves`, article forms) are logged as OQ-I027-3; labels use the walkthrough names, strings keep the ROM text.
- **Ad-hoc slip, reported:** to find the ROM change above I ran one-off `node -e` commands that temporarily rewrote `us/overrides.json` (the `71774` entry removed, then restored from a backup copy) and `us/mnemonics.json` (subsets of entries, then restored from a backup copy) plus a small shell loop, `/tmp/bis.sh`, around them. They touched no notes, names, comments, `blocks.json` or docs, and both files were restored byte-for-byte before the single hand edit that removed `player_name`. No tool exists for mnemonics and overrides, so a bisect option in the baserom tools would be a sensible later addition (not done).
