## Findings

- **Mnemonic evidence came from the consumers.** Examples: `joy_release_latch` is cleared with `TRB` by its users (a held button acts once); `$1B70`/`$1B74` are the sum of the weapon and armor table entries (`code_011EED`); `$0332` bits are shifted out by the HUD routine; the enemy gauge readers exist (`code_012659`, the gauge string).
- **`joy_override`** is the name used for `$0468` (FM-A1 called it `forced_joy`); notes, comments and mnemonics use the one name.
- **Naming:** `UploadScrollStrips` (A4) and `SpawnLairMonster` (A2) are other batches' names, so R19 and R52 got different names.
- **Not applied:** mnemonics for `$0372`, `$039A`, `$1A02`, `$1A04` and the direct-page scratch (`OQ-I023-3`); the `M = 1` overrides of `OQ-I005-7` (`OQ-I023-5`).
- No DISCREPANCY rows are new; the three in `OQ-I005-1/2/3` (Super Bracelet, Critical Sword, Psycho Sword) are cited in the notes by ID.
