## Findings and corrections

- **Scroll model written down once** (`lib_map_streaming` block note): BG1 and BG2 maps are 16x16 metatiles (1 byte each, 256-byte pages) with 8-byte definitions; bit 9 of a tile word is the solid flag of that quarter and the four flags form the low nibble of `tile_type_map`. Per frame: `AimCameraAtPlayer`, `ScrollCameraByDelta` (request bits in `scroll_requests`), `BuildScrollStrips` (258-byte strip records at `$7E3000`), `UploadScrollStrips` by DMA channel 2 in the NMI.
- **Correction to the engine notes:** `code_0109D6` was described as a 'return-point store'. It is `ReleaseLairAtSwitch`; the return trip is only a branch of it, and `return_active` has a second source in the mixed table `unk14_017504`. The three mentions in `engine_boot_main_loop` were reworded.
- **Lair record** (32 bytes, `data_00BA0D`): the field layout and the two release cases (opens a path, frees an NPC; the second may send the player to the NPC's home through `LocationAreaScene`) are in the block note of `lib_tile_triggers_and_lair_return`. `data_00BA0D` itself has no part note yet.
- **Slabs:** alternative full-size copies of the map stored behind the live map; `CopyMapRectFromSlab` copies a rectangle from slab k, keeping the live collision high nibble; the two staging copies replace the whole byte.
- **Animation records:** `NextFrameRecord` / `CopyFrameData` write to `$7F:$0A` and add `$200` when the count byte is 2; this matches the palette shadow and `coldata_buffer`, so the I025 'sprite animation' wording was changed to 'copy' (OQ-I026-3).
- **Mis-decoded code:** `BuildScrollStrips` (offset 65803) lacks `M = 1`; the two `JSR` operands are literals in the rebuilt ROM. Not fixed here because the fix changes the SHA-1 (OQ-I026-1, P1, your decision). The part note carries the real disassembly.
- **Walkthrough check:** NPC names and the lair and release stories were read against the walkthrough chapters. No new mismatch beyond the spelling points of OQ-I008-14 and OQ-I008-15 (OQ-I026-11).
- **Ad-hoc slip, reported:** while bisecting the decode problem of `BuildScrollStrips` a one-off `node -e` command rewrote `us/overrides.json`. It touched no notes, names, comments or block files; the file was restored and then fixed with the editor tool (only the `67277` change above remains).
